WHY WECARE EXISTS

Distance shouldn't mean losing control.

For an NRI, a vacant home can create worry: leaks, electrical faults, theft risks, overgrown gardens, repairs and small problems becoming expensive ones.

And when parents or family need help, arranging everything from another country can be stressful.

⌂Empty-home riskRegular supervision reduces the chance that small issues go unnoticed.
