FAQs

Questions you may have.

Who is WeCare for?

NRIs, families living outside Kerala and property owners who need reliable local support.

Can I choose how often my property is visited?

Yes. Visit frequency can be selected according to your requirement and care plan.

Will I receive photos?

Photo/video updates can be included with property inspection services.

Can I request only one service?

Yes. You can contact us for individual services as well as recurring care.

Can you coordinate repairs?

Yes. We can coordinate agreed maintenance requirements such as plumbing, electrical, painting and other home services.

Can you support my parents?

We provide practical local assistance and family support based on the service you choose.
