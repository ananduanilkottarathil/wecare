FAQS

Questions? We've got you.

Don't see your question here? Talk to us.

Who is WeCare for?

WeCare is designed especially for NRIs, families living outside Kerala, second-home owners and anyone who needs a reliable local partner.

Can you manage a property when I am abroad?

Yes. We can coordinate scheduled checks, cleaning, maintenance, repairs and other agreed property-care tasks while keeping you informed.

Do you provide elder care?

We provide practical support and assistance services. The exact scope is agreed for each family. WeCare is not a replacement for clinical or emergency medical care.

Can I request a one-time service?

Yes. Depending on the requirement and service area, we can support one-time tasks as well as recurring care plans.

How do I get started?

Send us a WhatsApp message or request a consultation. Tell us your location and what you need, and we'll guide you through the next step.
